Teacher's Note

Designed with Purpose

When I worked as a High School Spanish Teacher at Orange County Public Schools in Virginia, I noticed a recurring hurdle for my students: Spanish verb conjugation is notoriously challenging to master through traditional worksheets alone.

To give my students the extra edge they needed, I created this interactive platform as a supplemental assignment tool. It bridges the gap between classroom instruction and independent practice, transforming complex verb charts into an engaging, gamified experience complete with instant validation and confidence-boosting safeguards.
